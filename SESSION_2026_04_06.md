### 2. Critical Bug Found & Fixed ✅

**Bug**: Network config requests were being sent, but OK responses were being rejected with "OK packet not expected".

**Root Cause**: The `send_network_config_request` callback wasn't tracking expected replies.

**Fix**: Added `node.expectReplyTo(packet_id)` call in `src/node/node.zig:1792-1794`

**Impact**: Network config responses will now be accepted and processed when received from authorized networks.
